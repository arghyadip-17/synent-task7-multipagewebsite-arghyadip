About us

We Exist To Close The Skills Gap

Learnova was founded in 2019 by engineers and educators frustrated by the disconnect between traditional education and the real needs of the tech industry. Today we help 50,000+ learners worldwide build career-ready skills.

Our mission

To make world-class tech education accessible to everyone — regardless of where they live, what they studied, or their financial situation. Skills shouldn't be gated by privilege.

Our method

We believe in learning by doing. Every lesson connects directly to a real-world task. Projects are reviewed by industry professionals, not automated graders.

Our community

Over 50,000 learners in our Slack and Discord. Study groups, peer code reviews, hackathons, and job referrals — because great careers are built on great networks.

Our promise

We stand behind our curriculum. Complete a flagship program and don't land a job within 6 months? You get a full refund. We win only when you win.
